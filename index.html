How to play PARADOX

Every day, a new secret word is waiting for you. Five hints describe it—but exactly one hint is false.

  1. Read the 3 structural hints and 2 hints about the word's meaning.
  2. Select the hint you believe is false and enter your word guess.
  3. The game checks your selected hint first. If it is wrong, you lose one attempt. If it is correct, the game checks your word.
  4. Correctly identify the false hint and the word to win. You have 5 attempts.

Each failed submission crosses out one square. Solve daily to build your streak. A new puzzle arrives at midnight GMT+7.
